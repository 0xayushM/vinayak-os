# Onboarding a company

1. **Workspace** — create it (Settings → new brand) inside the group.
2. **Source** — TranzAct: enter credentials, Test, Sync all (history walks in the background). Tally: install the bridge on the accounts PC and pair it (when available).
3. **Profile** — Business Brain page: industry, fiscal year, GST, healthy margin, seasonality, key customers and terms, KPIs the owner watches.
4. **People** — add each user; they pick what they do on first login; an owner grants "approve messages" / "approve money" in Settings → People.
5. **Contacts and costs** — accountant uploads customer email/WhatsApp and cost per SKU (CSV).
6. **Reconcile** — headline totals vs the source's own report; review Sync → ingest issues.
7. **Switch on** — enable the first watchers (overdue rungs, data stale, anomalies) on confirm; the daily brief starts next morning.
8. **Record** — add the company page under `companies/` with workspace id, source, contacts, quirks.
